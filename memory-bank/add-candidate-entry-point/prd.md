# PRD: Add Candidate Entry Point

## Summary
Enable recruiters to easily find and use the "Add Candidate" action from the dashboard, launching the candidate creation workflow.

## Goals
- Provide a clear, accessible button or link labeled "Add Candidate" on the recruiter dashboard.
- Ensure the entry point meets accessibility standards (WCAG 2.1 AA).
- Open the candidate creation form without a full page reload.

## Acceptance Criteria
- "Add Candidate" button/link is visible and prominent on the dashboard.
- Control meets contrast and focus requirements.
- Clicking the control opens the candidate form (modal or page) via client-side navigation.
